**Perspectives to explore:** {{perspectives}}

For each perspective listed, analyze the topic through that lens:

**[Perspective N]: [Name]**
- Core interests and priorities
- How they view this topic
- What they stand to gain or lose
- Key concerns or objections
- What they would recommend

After covering all perspectives, identify:
- Where the perspectives **align**
- Where they **conflict**
- The **blind spots** that no single perspective captures alone
- A **synthesis recommendation** that accounts for all viewpoints
